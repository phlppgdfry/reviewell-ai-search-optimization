### [00:30 - 03:00] The Death of the 10 Blue Links
**ALEX:**  
Sarah, let’s explain what changed. When someone asks ChatGPT or Perplexity for the best solution in an industry, how does the model decide who to cite?

**SARAH:**  
Traditional SEO was about keywords and backlinks. But Generative Engines operate on **Citation Authority and Semantic Consensus**. When an LLM generates a response, it performs Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). It scrapes high-trust structured nodes, checks `/llms.txt` and Schema.org documentation, and evaluates sentiment across directories. If your website has technical AEO architecture—structured JSON-LD, extractable policy matrices, and zero negative review contamination—the AI model cites you as the authoritative source.

### [03:00 - 06:30] The 6 Core AEO KPIs Every Founder Must Track
**ALEX:**  
In Reviewell's AEO platform, you don't look at keyword rankings anymore. You track 6 distinct metrics. Can you walk us through those?

**SARAH:**  
Absolutely. In modern Answer Engine Optimization, we monitor:
1. **AI Visibility Score:** The percentage of times your brand appears when commercial prompt queries are executed.
2. **Share of Voice (SOV):** How often you are cited compared to your top three legacy competitors.
3. **Citation Share:** Whether the AI engine actually hyperlinks your domain as the primary reference source.
4. **Referral Traffic via AI:** Direct click-throughs originating from Perplexity, ChatGPT, and Claude.
5. **Conversion Rate of AI Visitors:** AI traffic converts at 3 to 4 times higher rates than traditional search because the user was already qualified by the model.
6. **Qualified Recommendation Sentiment:** Ensuring the engine's summary paints your company as the premier market leader without disclaimers.
